# PipLearn Product Launch — Storyboard

Format: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 36 seconds. Creative mode: autonomous free creation.

## Product brief

PipLearn is a premium software-engineering learning platform that teaches through execution, persistence, and human calibration. The launch film follows the real learner journey shown by the application: begin with the Python foundation manual, move into the nine-phase Terminal Ledger project, capture lessons in the Problem Journal, and defend key decisions with human mentors.

## Visual and motion direction

- Brand source: the existing PipLearn UI—electric sky blue, crystalline glass objects, alpine environments, editorial white serif headlines, restrained sans-serif support copy, and the existing blue stacked-layer logo.
- Motion personality: calm, premium, optimistic. Long camera eases, subtle perspective, readable holds, one main action per scene.
- Typography: Georgia/editorial serif for launch lines; system sans and monospace for support labels, matching the application.
- Data policy: only public demo content already visible in this local project is shown. No customer, account, secret, or live data is introduced.
- Audio: restrained tech-house bed with cinematic whooshes and a riser → impact → sparkle outro. A no-BGM version is rendered from the same timeline.

## Feature-to-shot mapping

| Product feature | Primary recipe | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Brand promise and home experience | `crane-rise-reveal`-informed camera adaptation | Moves from a crystalline detail into the full real home page—detail to system, without claiming the recipe's row-pulse mechanics. |
| Foundation curriculum | `document-typewriter-reveal` camera/readability adaptation | Uses the recipe's title-close-to-full-page camera pacing on the real manual; it deliberately does not claim the recipe's DOM typing/caret implementation. |
| Nine-phase project workflow | `scroll-brake-moves` speed-contrast adaptation | Uses a fast visual handoff that lands on the real active phase; it deliberately omits the recipe's long DOM list and reticle. |
| Problem Journal and mentor checkpoints | Independent page-camera scenes | Keeps both content-heavy real pages legible instead of forcing the denser recipe action. |
| Final brand memory | `outro-group-photo-launch` | Reunites real page fragments around the PipLearn wordmark. |
| Scene handoffs | `shot-transitions · flash-cut` | Maintains continuity between the distinct brand environments. |
